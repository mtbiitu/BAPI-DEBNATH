Institutional Fraud Protection

6-Stage Anti-Proxy Security Fusion

Buddy punching, stolen cards, pre-recorded audio spoofing, and remote proxy attendance are systematically eliminated through layered hardware and neural checks.

Stage 1

Physical Multi-Sensor Gating

Monitors dwell-time latency between RFID card tap and optical fingerprint scan. Anomalous or automated transitions (<300ms or >15000ms) instantly trigger manual administrative inspection.

Sub-Second Optical Validation
Stage 2

BLE RSSI Proximity Gating

Mobile check-in requests are gated by a secondary ESP32 BLE peripheral. The client device must verify a minimum -75 dBm RSSI threshold (<3m physical radius) and solve a salted SHA-256 challenge PIN.

Prevents GPS Spoofing
Stage 3

Edge I2S Direct Audio DMA

Upon physical biometric trigger, the primary ESP32 records a 9-second 16kHz 16-bit PCM voice packet from the INMP441 microphone directly into non-volatile storage, validating exact 44-byte RIFF headers.

Bit-Perfect Audio Pipeline
Stage 4

Whisper ASR Phrase Presence

Audio is evaluated by Whisper ASR (leveraging Apple Silicon Neural Engine on macOS via MLX). Rejects empty, muffled, or silence-injected files if no_speech_prob > 0.85.

Speech Verification Passed
Stage 5

SpeechBrain ECAPA-TDNN Embedding

Extracts a 192-dimensional speaker embedding vector from the audio stream and calculates the Cosine Similarity score against enrolled centroids. Requires a rigorous score >= 0.25.

192-Dim Neural Cosine Match
Stage 6

Cross-Speaker Daily Clustering

If an imposter attempts to speak on behalf of an absent peer, the server cross-compares all audio uploaded today in a pairwise similarity matrix. Voice reuse triggers immediate revocation and SMS dispatch.

Zero-Tolerance Proxy Hunter

Industrial CAD Design

Autodesk Fusion 360 Enclosure Engineering

The device terminal is housed inside a custom-engineered enclosure built with parametric 3D CAD modeling. It integrates bespoke acoustic waveguides, precise sensor retention brackets, and internal battery power management.

  • Ergonomic Biometric Fingerprint Recess

    Angled mount cradles the R307 optical prism, ensuring natural finger placement and consistent contact pressure across varied hand sizes.

  • Acoustic Port & Waveguide Optimization

    Tapered front acoustic opening channels voice signals directly to the INMP441 MEMS element while attenuating lateral room reflections.

  • IP2326 2S Balance Charger Tray

    Dedicated internal bay houses a 2-cell Lithium-Ion battery management board for continuous operation during localized grid outages.
